GreenDIGIT Commons Initiative

This page is created to catalogue, maintain, and operate the outcomes of the GreenDIGIT project beyond the project timeline. GreenDIGIT has produced important architectural, policy, impact analysis, and monitoring and optimisation tools for Research Infrastructures (RIs). The Commons brings them together in one place, with clear ownership, support levels, and a community of users who keep them in use.

How we sustain the outcomes

The Commons is a lightweight, partner-driven commitment, not a new project. Every listed outcome follows these four commitments.

  1. Clear ownership

    Each tool has a named maintaining institution, contact person, and declared support level.

  2. Durable homes

    Code lives in a shared repository, releases get Zenodo DOIs and are archived in Software Heritage, and services are listed in the EOSC catalogue.

  3. Continued use

    Ambassadors run tutorials, collect feedback, and connect tools to new RIs, projects, and communities.

  4. Onward funding

    Partners build on Commons tools in new proposals and report their reuse in Horizon Europe exploitation plans.

Get involved

Whether you operate an RI, develop tools, or fund research infrastructure, there is a way to take part.

Use a tool

Try a tool from the catalogue and tell us how it worked for your infrastructure. Use cases help us prioritise.

Become an ambassador

Champion one or more tools in your community, run a training session, or co-author a use-case report.

Maintain or extend

Contribute code, documentation, or integrations, or take over maintenance of a tool within your organisation.
